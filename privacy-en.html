Privacy policy

Orders and customer service

For WhatsApp orders, the store receives your full name, phone number, delivery address, items ordered and delivery notes. We use them to process orders, check stock, contact you, coordinate payment and delivery or pickup, and provide customer service. The owner and authorized employees handle orders. Store staff make deliveries. WhatsApp/Meta provides the communication channel.

The store keeps printed copies of receipts and tax invoices. Card payments are handled through BOSS register software connected to a payment processor. For questions about how your transaction records are kept, contact us using the details below.

Automatic daily WhatsApp Business backups are enabled. Chats are not deleted on a regular schedule. For retention details or to request deletion, contact the privacy representative below.

Optional site analytics

Site analytics is disabled in this version. The catalog, cart and WhatsApp ordering are available. The details below describe how analytics will work if it is enabled in a future version and you choose to consent.

Charlie Boutique asks permission to measure site use to understand which products interest visitors and improve shopping. If you decline, you can still browse the catalog, use the cart and order through WhatsApp.

After consent, we measure visits, product views, cart additions and clicks to open WhatsApp. Events include receipt time, broad device and referral categories, and, where applicable, a product identifier or WhatsApp link type. A click does not confirm an order. Events exclude names, residential addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, order and message content, IP addresses and complete referring page URLs.

Preferences and browser identifier

Accessibility and entry language preferences stay in the browser. Accessibility settings are not sent to analytics and do not enable analytics. Where purchase terms acceptance is available, only the accepted version and time according to your device are stored locally. The record remembers your choice and is neither sent to analytics nor treated as conclusive proof of identity or acceptance.

Your browser remembers your choice until you change it or clear site data. A random browser identifier is created only after consent to distinguish visits and reduce duplicates. It expires after 90 days and is replaced on return if you still consent. A protected representation is stored on the server. Choose no analytics or withdraw anytime under Privacy preferences in the footer. Withdrawal stops new events and clears the local analytics identifier. Earlier data remains subject to the retention periods below.

Retention, access and providers

Detailed analytics are retained for 90 measurement days in Israel time and daily summaries for 365 days without browser identifiers. Authorized administrators can access reports. Supabase provides the analytics infrastructure and database, whose production region is eu-west-1. GitHub Pages hosts the storefront. Cloudflare Pages hosts the separate store administration system. A browser identifier does not reliably identify a person. Provider operational logs may include IP addresses separately from analytics tables.

GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security even while store analytics is disabled. The GitHub privacy statement describes processing that may take place in the United States.
